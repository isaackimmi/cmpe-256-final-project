How the 5 Parquet Tables Connect

Every row in travel_behaviors.parquet is one user's Home City History (c_h) paired with what they actually visited in a Destination City (c_o).

travel_behaviors 518.6K rows · 144 MB
  • user_id (BIGINT)
  • r_h → home region QID
  • r_o → dest region QID
  • n_home_ci / n_travel_ci
  • c_h[] home visits list
  • c_o[] dest visits (LABEL)
pois.parquet 687.2K rows · 103 MB
  • fsq_place_id (PK)
  • locality → region QID
  • google_name, venue_schema
  • google_categories[]
  • google_rating, n_reviews
  • n_checkins, n_users_visited
metadata_all 687.2K rows · 148 MB
  • fsq_place_id (PK)
  • fsq_name, fsq_country
  • fsq_latitude / longitude
  • fsq_category_labels[]
  • fsq_date_closed (filter!)
  • google_* mirrored fields
region_labels 1,173 rows · 19 KB
  • region_id (Wikidata QID)
  • city_name (e.g. Madrid)
  • country_name (e.g. Spain)
  • + place_attributes (28.8K):
  • hours_week, price, desc
Verified Join Key: UNNEST(travel.c_h).venue_id = pois.fsq_place_id — both keep the exact foursquare:<24-hex> prefix (do NOT strip it!).

5 Data Gotchas We Already Caught (Gold for Your KDD & Pandera Report!)

Gotcha #1
Transit & Private Home Noise

TrainStation (1.22M ci), Airport (305K), House (19.2K POIs), ApartmentComplex (16.1K), and City must be excluded from recommended targets—otherwise you recommend train stations!

Gotcha #2
venue_category Is a Hex ID

venue_category stores Foursquare hex IDs (4bf58dd8...). Use venue_schema, google_categories[], and fsq_category_labels[] for readable text.

Gotcha #3
33,877 Closed POIs (4.9%)

metadata_all.fsq_date_closed flags permanently closed venues. Keep them in historical training features if desired, but filter them out at recommendation time.

Gotcha #4
23.5% Missing Google Ratings

161.6K POIs have NULL ratings. Use Bayesian smoothing toward the category/city mean (global mean = 3.98★) + a binary has_google_rating indicator.

Gotcha #5
Metro Rollups & Raw QIDs

Some regions are metro rollups (Osaka includes Kyoto/Kobe) and a few labels are raw QIDs (Q49355990). Map/filter clean metro names for user-facing city pickers.

Gotcha #6
User Leakage Risk in CV

1 user visits 3.49 destination cities on average (up to 104!). Always split 5-fold CV by user_id, never randomly by trip row.

Copy-Paste Starter Query (Try It in 2 Seconds!)

Run this with .venv/bin/python to inspect clean museums & attractions in any city:

import duckdb
con = duckdb.connect()
con.sql("""
  SELECT COALESCE(p.google_name, m.fsq_name) AS name,
         r.city_name, p.venue_schema, p.google_rating, p.n_checkins
  FROM 'data/tripworld/pois.parquet' p
  JOIN 'data/tripworld/metadata/metadata_all.parquet' m USING (fsq_place_id)
  JOIN 'data/tripworld/region_labels.parquet' r ON p.locality = r.region_id
  WHERE r.city_name IN ('London', 'Madrid', 'Amsterdam')
    AND p.venue_schema IN ('schema:Museum', 'schema:ArtGallery')
    AND m.fsq_date_closed IS NULL
  ORDER BY p.n_checkins DESC
  LIMIT 10
""").show()
